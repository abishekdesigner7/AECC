## 4 · Critical flags (resolve before more build-out)
1. **Industrial-vs-Home positioning (biggest).** The official company is **heavy-industrial / mining / oil-&-gas / Middle-East**. The website's entire **Home & Facility + Client Care** story (residential plumbing, AC, CCTV, solar) is **absent from the official profile**. Either (a) the company is genuinely adding a home-services arm (then the deck is just out of date), or (b) the website drifted off-brief and should pivot to the real industrial/mining identity. **Ask the client.**
2. **Domain & email split:** `aecc.in` + `info@aecc.in` (site) vs `antrosengineering.com` + `aeccngl@gmail.com` (profile). Confirm the canonical pair before printing it anywhere.
3. **"Gulf Asia Contracting Company"** on slides 22–24 — identify what this is (Oman entity? partner? leftover) before referencing.
4. **No ISO certifications evidenced** — don't add ISO badges without the actual certificates.
